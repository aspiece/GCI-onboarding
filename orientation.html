Course Orientation

What you need to know before your first assignment.

Where Assignments Are Posted

All assignments are posted in Google Classroom. Check your class stream and the “Classwork” tab every day for new tasks and deadlines.

How Assignments Are Submitted

Submit all work through the platform where it was assigned. Do not submit via email unless your instructor specifically directs you to. Follow the instructions on each assignment for how to turn in your work.

What To Do When Absent

When you are absent:

  1. Check Google Classroom for missed assignments.
  2. Complete work by the make-up deadline posted in the class.
  3. Contact your instructor if you have questions about what you missed.
  4. Do not wait for your instructor to remind you.

How To Ask for Help

  1. Try solving it yourself for at least 5 minutes.
  2. Ask a classmate at your table.
  3. Search online for the error message or concept.
  4. Raise your hand or message your instructor in the LMS.
  5. Use the Help page to report technical problems.

Equipment Expectations

  • Bring your Chromebook (or school-issued device) charged every day.
  • Report hardware problems to the Help Desk immediately.
  • Keep your device clean and in its case when not in use.
  • Do not install unauthorized software.
  • Use only approved apps and websites during class time.

What To Do When Technology Fails

  1. Read the error message carefully.
  2. Try signing out and signing back in with your school account.
  3. Refresh the page or restart the app.
  4. Try a different browser if available.
  5. Ask a classmate if they are having the same issue.
  6. Report the problem using the Help page and continue with other work.

Appropriate AI Use

AI tools can be useful learning aids when used responsibly. In this course:

  • You may use AI to help understand concepts.
  • You may not use AI to complete assignments for you unless your instructor specifically permits it.
  • Submitting AI-generated work as your own without permission is academic dishonesty.
  • When in doubt, ask your instructor before using an AI tool.

How Grades Work

  • Grades are updated in your LMS and the school grade portal.
  • Assignments are graded on completeness, correctness, and effort.
  • Late work policies vary by assignment. Check each assignment for details.
  • Contact your instructor if you believe a grade is incorrect.
