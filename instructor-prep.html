Instructor Prep

Use this quick checklist before students begin onboarding.

Before Students Start

  • Confirm students are enrolled in the correct Computer Science course.
  • Confirm first-year students are enrolled in Career Essentials.
  • Confirm second-year students are enrolled in Career Exploration.
  • Have each student's name, student ID, and password ready to give to the student in person.
  • Give students their GCI student ID and password before directing them to the contact information form.
  • Have workstation assignments ready for courses that require technical setup.
  • Do not post or send student passwords through Google Classroom, Teams, or email.
Open Progress Sheet

Only GCI Computer Science staff should have access to the Progress Sheet and Setup Check sheet.

Google Classroom Post

Create one visible post in each Computer Science course and allow students to comment.

Suggested post: Welcome to class. Reply with your preferred first name, one technology skill you already have, one thing you hope to learn this year, and one question you have about the course.

Open Google Classroom

Students should not share passwords, student IDs, phone numbers, addresses, or other private information in their comment.

During Onboarding

  • Keep Microsoft Teams open while students complete the setup checklist.
  • Watch for student chat messages that confirm they are signed in and ready for class.
  • React to or reply to each student message so the student knows the message was received.
  • Use Teams to respond to student onboarding questions when they need support.
Open Microsoft Teams

Setup Check Review

Use the Setup Check sheet to confirm students finished the onboarding process and identify anyone who needs follow-up.

  • Look for students who submitted the setup check after completing the onboarding steps.
  • Review their readiness status: ready, almost ready, or help needed.
  • Check notes for missing access, incorrect course enrollment, software issues, or account problems.
  • Follow up with students marked almost ready or help needed before they begin independent work.
