Need Help?

Follow these steps before reporting a problem.

Before you report a problem, try these steps:

  1. 1
    Read the error message. It often tells you exactly what is wrong. Write it down or take a screenshot.
  2. 2
    Verify you are using your school account. Sign out and sign back in with your school Google account (not a personal account).
  3. 3
    Ask someone at your table. A classmate may have seen the same issue.
  4. 4
    Refresh the page or sign in again. Close the tab, reopen the site, and try once more.
  5. 5
    Still stuck? Report the problem using the button below.

Report a Problem

Let your instructor know you are experiencing a technical problem. Be as specific as possible and include the tool, page, or error message you are seeing.

After reporting the problem, continue with anything else you can complete. Your instructor will follow up with you.

Common Issues

  • Can’t sign in to Google Classroom: Make sure you’re using your school email, not a personal account.
  • CodeHS not loading: Try a different browser or clear your browser cache.
  • AP Computer Science A GitHub login issue: Confirm your username and password, or use the “Forgot password” link.
  • Chromebook running slowly: Close unnecessary tabs and restart your device.
  • Assignment not showing up: Check the course platform your instructor told you to use, and confirm you joined the correct class.
  • Forgot a link: Return to the Start Here portal. All course links are there.

When You Arrive Each Day

  1. Log in to your school account.
  2. Open your course.
  3. Check today’s instructions.
  4. Begin working.

Starting independently shows your instructor you’re ready to learn.
